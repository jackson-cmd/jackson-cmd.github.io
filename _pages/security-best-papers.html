Every paper that won a Best Paper or Distinguished Paper award at the four top security conferences: IEEE S&P, USENIX Security, ACM CCS and NDSS, from {{ years | last }} to {{ years | first }}. Each entry links to the paper, and each award is checked against the conference's own award page.

Award papers are a good model for your own work: they show what program committees reward in each area. Filter the list to your topic and to the type of paper you are writing, read a few of them, and compare how they state the problem, the threat model and the evidence. The guide below lists what award papers of each type tend to have in common.

Disclaimer

  • An award is one committee's judgment in one year. Many of the most influential security papers never won one, and award practices differ between the four conferences and over time (for example, some years give one Best Paper and others give twenty Distinguished Papers), so counts are not comparable across venues or years.
  • Topics and types of paper are automatic estimates from titles and can be wrong; a paper often fits more than one.
  • Award lists on conference websites sometimes change after the event or disappear when a site moves. If an entry is wrong or missing, please send an email (the address is on the homepage); it will be fixed in the next update.
  • This page is not affiliated with the conferences or their sponsors (IEEE, USENIX, ACM SIGSAC, the Internet Society), and they do not endorse it.
