ℹ️ How to read this calendar & data sources

Each conference appears under its typical deadline month, grouped by CORE tier (ICORE 2026). The typical month is the most common final-cycle full-paper deadline over the last three editions (ccf-deadlines dataset; ARR commitment steps excluded).

Multi-cycle venues are placed under their main (final) cycle — e.g. USENIX Security in January, IEEE S&P in November, ACM CCS in April. CCF classes follow the CCF 2026 directory (7th edition). DRM and ILP are omitted (no recent public deadline data). Acceptance rates are estimated from the last three editions with published numbers. New venues not yet in the CCF directory (e.g. IEEE SaTML) are marked NEW.

Data sources: ccf.atom.im (CCF 2026, 7th ed.)  •  portal.core.edu.au (ICORE 2026)  •  ccf-deadlines (GitHub)
